PUZZLE TIMER · MIDNIGHT LAUNCH

Default: 90 seconds. Start begins the countdown. Stop freezes it and shows time
used. Resume continues; Reset restores the selected duration for the next player.
At zero, the display reads TIMES UP. Adjust time offers presets or a custom duration
from 1 second to 99:59. The chosen duration is remembered on that device.

GITHUB PAGES
Upload everything in this folder to your repository's Pages source folder.
Keep index.html at that folder's root, with assets/ alongside it. No build step,
package installation, or external font/network dependency is required.

IPAD HOME SCREEN
Open your GitHub Pages URL in Safari, then Share > Add to Home Screen.
The disco-ball PNG is the Apple touch icon and manifest icon. After one online
visit has finished caching, the app also opens offline. Open the hosted URL once
after publishing updates to refresh the cache. It requests that the screen stay
awake while running, where supported. Keep the timer in the foreground during play.

Directly opening index.html also runs the timer, without offline installation.
